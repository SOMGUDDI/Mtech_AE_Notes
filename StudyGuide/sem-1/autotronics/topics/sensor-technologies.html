Automotive Sensor Technologies

IntermediateExam importance ★★★★★Sensors

Quick overview

Sensor technologies transform a physical quantity into an electrical signal that the vehicle can process.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

Lectures classify sensors by the technology and the measurement, with resistive, inductive and capacitive behaviour used as important foundations. Hall-effect and oxygen-sensor discussions are also present.
