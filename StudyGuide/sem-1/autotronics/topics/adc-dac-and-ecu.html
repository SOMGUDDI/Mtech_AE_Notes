ADC, DAC and ECU Interfaces

IntermediateExam importance ★★★★★Signal Conversion

Quick overview

ADC converts an analogue signal to digital data for the ECU; DAC or equivalent output stages support analogue actuation where required.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

Lecture 1 identifies ADC as analogue-to-digital conversion before the signal is sent to the ECU. Later lectures repeat the complete chain including DAC and actuator.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

Sampling rate must capture signal dynamics; resolution sets the smallest voltage step. These specifications must match the control objective, sensor range and noise level.

Workflow / system view

Analogue sensor output
ADC sampling / quantisation
ECU algorithm
DAC or output driver
Actuator

Worked engineering connection

Example: A throttle-position voltage sampled by an ADC becomes a numerical input for torque-control software.
