Automotive Sensor-to-Actuator Loop

BeginnerExam importance ★★★★★System Foundations

Quick overview

Automotive electronic control closes the loop from a physical quantity to sensing, computation and actuation.

Lecture material repeatedly builds the chain: measured quantity → sensor → signal conditioning → ADC → ECU → DAC → actuator, with feedback creating a closed loop where applicable.
