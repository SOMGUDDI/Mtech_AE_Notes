Powertrain Architecture

IntermediateExam importance ★★★★★Powertrain Fundamentals

Quick overview

The powertrain transfers and adapts energy from a prime mover to the driven wheels.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

Lectures 2 and 3 trace the chain through clutch or coupling, gearbox, shafts and wheels, and discuss how component placement affects packaging and serviceability.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

Gear ratios trade wheel torque against wheel speed. Architecture choices also affect centre tunnel space, mass distribution and driveline losses.

Workflow / system view

Engine / motor
Clutch or coupling
Transmission
Final drive / shafts
Driven wheels

Worked engineering connection

Example: A low gear multiplies wheel torque for launch but limits vehicle speed at a given engine speed.
