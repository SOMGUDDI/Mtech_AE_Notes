Tractive Force and Road Loads

IntermediateExam importance ★★★★★Vehicle Fundamentals

Quick overview

Tractive force at the tyre-road contact patch must overcome road loads and provide acceleration.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

Lecture 2 defines tractive force as the force obtained at wheel-and-road contact after powertrain losses, then develops the related technical terms.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

A useful longitudinal model is Ftractive = Frolling + Faero + Fgrade + ma. It exposes why speed, mass, slope and tyre behaviour all affect propulsion demand.

Workflow / system view

Prime mover torque
Transmission losses
Wheel torque
Tyre-road contact
Tractive force / motion

Worked engineering connection

Example: At steady speed on level ground, acceleration is zero; traction must only balance rolling and aerodynamic resistance.
