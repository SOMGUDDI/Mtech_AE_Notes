Chassis and Body

BeginnerExam importance ★★★★Vehicle Structure

Quick overview

The chassis provides the structural skeleton; the body protects occupants and packages the vehicle systems.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

Lecture 5 uses the human skeleton-and-skin analogy: the chassis accommodates powertrain, suspension, steering and brakes, while the body provides enclosure and protection.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

Structure must balance stiffness, crashworthiness, mass, manufacturability and access for service. A body-in-white is therefore more than cosmetic sheet metal.

Worked engineering connection

Example: A battery-electric vehicle may integrate its battery pack into the underbody, making structural design and crash load paths especially important.
