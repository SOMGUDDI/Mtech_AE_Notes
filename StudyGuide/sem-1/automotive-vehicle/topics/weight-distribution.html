Weight Distribution and Vehicle Balance

IntermediateExam importance ★★★★Vehicle Structure

Quick overview

Weight distribution is how vehicle mass is shared between axles and wheels.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

The syllabus explicitly includes weight distribution and analysis in the chassis-and-body unit. The vehicle is first treated as a body before moving to individual components.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

Static distribution influences available tyre grip and handling balance; dynamic load transfer changes the normal load during braking, acceleration and cornering.

Worked engineering connection

Example: Moving a heavy battery pack low and near the wheelbase centre can lower the centre of gravity and reduce load-transfer effects.
