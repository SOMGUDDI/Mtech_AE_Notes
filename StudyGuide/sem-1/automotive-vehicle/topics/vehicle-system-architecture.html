Vehicle as an Engineering System

BeginnerExam importance ★★★★★Vehicle Fundamentals

Quick overview

A vehicle is an integrated system of structure, propulsion, control, steering, braking and body functions.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

The opening lecture frames this subject as the chassis of the degree: a foundation for understanding basic automotive components before their detailed design.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

Thinking in subsystems helps locate interfaces. For example, propulsion produces wheel torque, but chassis, tyres and road contact determine the resulting motion.
