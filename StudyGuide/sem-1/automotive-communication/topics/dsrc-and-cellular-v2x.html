DSRC and Cellular V2X

IntermediateExam importance ★★★★★V2X & Vehicular Networks

Quick overview

DSRC and cellular V2X are two technology paths for cooperative road communication.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

Lecture 3 identifies DSRC based on IEEE 802.11p and cellular V2X as the two technologies supporting V2X, with communication range discussed in the approximate 200–1000 m context.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

The key engineering choice is not just range. Latency, coverage, direct versus network-assisted communication, deployment cost and application safety requirements must all be considered.
