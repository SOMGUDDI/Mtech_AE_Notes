V2X Communication Overview

IntermediateExam importance ★★★★★V2X & Vehicular Networks

Quick overview

Vehicle-to-everything communication extends a vehicle's awareness beyond its own radar, camera, and LiDAR sensors.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

The lectures introduce V2V, V2I, V2P and V2N as the V2X family, connecting vehicles with other road users, infrastructure and networks for safer, cooperative mobility.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

V2X does not replace on-board perception. It adds cooperative information such as a hazard message from a vehicle outside the camera line of sight.
