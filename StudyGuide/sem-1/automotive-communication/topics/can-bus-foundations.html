CAN Bus Foundations

IntermediateExam importance ★★★★★In-Vehicle Networks

Quick overview

Controller Area Network (CAN) is a broadcast bus used to exchange messages among ECUs.

Lecture notes

📘 Sourced lecture synthesis

The laboratory discussion describes multiple ECUs connected to a CAN bus, CAN as a broadcasting protocol, and the role of 120-ohm impedance matching. It also flags CAN frames as essential background.

Core concept

💡 Additional explanation

CAN messages are identified by an identifier rather than a destination address. In a correctly terminated high-speed CAN bus, two 120-ohm terminators sit at the physical ends of the trunk.
